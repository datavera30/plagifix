FAQ
Common questions.

Does PlagiFix remove both plagiarism and AI detection flags?

Yes. PlagiFix addresses the specific document issues that cause both similarity detection flags and AI writing detection flags in a single processing run.

Will my document content change?

No. Only invisible elements are removed. Your visible text, formatting, images, tables, footnotes, and references remain exactly as you wrote them.

Is my document secure and private?

Yes. Your document is processed in memory and permanently deleted immediately after your download. We never store, read, or share your documents.

How do credits work?

Each credit gives you one document fix (.docx) or one AI & Plag Score. Credits never expire, and they stay valid from the date of purchase.

Which file formats are supported?

Currently .docx (Microsoft Word) files only. Support for additional formats is coming soon.

What if my document still has issues after processing?

Contact us at support@plagifix.com. We will reprocess your file or issue a full refund with no questions asked.

Is AI & Plag Score the same as the AI & Plagiarism Removal service?

No. AI & Plag Score gives you your Similarity and AI Writing scores, rather than removing flags from your document. Both share the same pricing and credits.

Do you store the document I upload for an AI & Plag Score?

No. Your document is only used to generate your score and is not kept in a database or long-term storage afterward.

How long does an AI & Plag Score take?

AI & Plag Scores are typically ready within a few hours. When many requests are waiting, it can take longer. We email you as soon as your scores are ready, and you can also see the status in your dashboard.

Why did I not get an AI score for my long document?

Turnitin does not generate an AI writing score for documents of more than 30,000 words. This is a limit set by Turnitin. You will still receive the Similarity score.

Where do I request an AI & Plag Score?

After signing in, go to the AI & Similarity Score tab in your dashboard, upload your document there and request your score.
