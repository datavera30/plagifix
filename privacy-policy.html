Privacy Policy

About this policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information PlagiFix collects, how we use it and how we protect it.

Information we collect

When you register, place an order or contact us, we collect your name and email address. For Instant Fix we collect the email address you enter.

How we use your information

We use your information to provide the service you asked for, to deliver your results by email and in your dashboard, to process payments and refunds, to answer support requests, and to keep the service safe and working well. We do not sell your information.

Your documents

We use your documents only to provide the service you asked for. For Instant Fix, your file is processed and the fixed file is not kept after it is sent to you. For AI & Plag Score, your uploaded file is deleted a few days after your score is sent. We keep your uploaded documents and related data only for as long as we need them to provide the service. This is usually a short time after processing, so you can download your result and get support. After that, they are securely deleted.

Payments

Payments are handled by our payment providers. We do not see or store your card or bank details.

Cookies

We use only the cookies that the website needs to work. We do not use advertising cookies.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and your documents from unauthorized access, change, disclosure or loss. Because no method of sending data over the internet or storing it electronically is completely secure, we cannot promise absolute security.
