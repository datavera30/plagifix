FAQ
Common questions.

Is this the same as PlagiFix's plagiarism removal service?

No. Turnitin Report is a separate, complementary feature with its own credits and pricing. It gives you an official report, rather than removing flags from your document.

Do you store my document?

Your document isn't kept in a database or long-term storage — it's only used to generate your report. Once your report is delivered, we don't retain a copy.

How long does a report take?

Reports are generated through Turnitin's institutional access and are typically ready within a few hours. You'll see the status update in your dashboard once it's ready.

Where do I request a report?

After signing in, go to the Turnitin Report tab in your dashboard — upload your document there and request your report.

Which file formats are supported?

Currently .docx (Microsoft Word) files only.

What if something's wrong with my report?

Contact us at support@plagifix.com and we'll review it.
