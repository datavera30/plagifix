What we remove
Every flag. Removed.

PlagiFix targets every known source of Turnitin integrity flags — both plagiarism and AI detection — in a single pass.

👁️

Hidden text removal

Removes invisible text embedded in your document that inflates word count and triggers Turnitin's similarity detection.

🎨

Invisible color correction

Detects and removes text colored to match the page background — a common source of both plagiarism and AI detection flags.

🔧

Formatting flag removal

Strips the specific formatting attributes in heading styles that Turnitin flags as AI writing detection violations.

📋

Metadata removal

Removes author name, institution, revision history, and timestamps from your document properties before submission.

🔢

Font size normalisation

Detects and corrects unusually small font sizes used to hide text — a known trigger for Turnitin's integrity checks.

🔗

Document repair

Fixes broken internal references that cause Microsoft Word's "unreadable content" warning when opening your file.

FAQ
Common questions.

Does PlagiFix remove both plagiarism and AI detection flags?

Yes. PlagiFix addresses the specific document issues that cause both similarity detection flags and AI writing detection flags — in a single processing run.

Will my document content change?

No. Only invisible elements are removed. Your visible text, formatting, images, tables, footnotes, and references remain exactly as you wrote them.

Is my document secure and private?

Yes. Your document is processed in memory and permanently deleted immediately after your download. We never store, read, or share your documents.

How do credits work?

Each credit processes one .docx document. Credits are valid for the duration shown on your plan — from 20 days to 90 days from the date of purchase.

Which file formats are supported?

Currently .docx (Microsoft Word) files only. Support for additional formats is coming soon.

What if my document still has issues after processing?

Contact us at support@plagifix.com. We will reprocess your file or issue a full refund — no questions asked.
