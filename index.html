FAQ
Common questions.

Does PlagiFix remove both plagiarism and AI detection flags?

Yes. PlagiFix addresses the specific document issues that cause both similarity detection flags and AI writing detection flags in a single processing run.

Will my document content change?

No. Only invisible elements are removed. Your visible text, formatting, images, tables, footnotes, and references remain exactly as you wrote them.

Is my document secure and private?

Yes. Your document is processed in memory and permanently deleted immediately after your download. We never store, read, or share your documents.

How do credits work?

Each credit processes one .docx document. Credits are valid for the duration shown on your plan, from 20 days to 90 days from the date of purchase. New accounts get 1 free credit valid for 30 days.

Which file formats are supported?

Currently .docx (Microsoft Word) files only. Support for additional formats is coming soon.

What if my document still has issues after processing?

Contact us at support@plagifix.com. We will reprocess your file or issue a full refund with no questions asked.

Is Turnitin Report the same as the Plagiarism & AI Removal service?

No. Turnitin Report gives you an official Similarity and AI Detection report, rather than removing flags from your document. Both share the same pricing and credits.

Do you store the document I upload for a Turnitin Report?

No. Your document is only used to generate your report and is not kept in a database or long-term storage afterward.

How long does a Turnitin Report take?

Reports are generated through Turnitin's institutional access and are typically ready within a few hours. You'll see the status update in your dashboard once it's ready.

Where do I request a Turnitin Report?

After signing in, go to the Turnitin Report tab in your dashboard — upload your document there and request your report.
