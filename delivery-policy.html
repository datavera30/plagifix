Delivery Policy

PlagiFix is a fully digital service. Nothing is sent by post or courier. Your result is delivered online once your payment is successful and your document has been processed.

How you receive your result

For Instant Fix, your fixed document is ready to download in your web browser as soon as processing is finished. For AI & Plag Score, your Similarity and AI Writing scores appear in your dashboard and are sent to the email address on your account.

Processing time

How long it takes can depend on the size of your document, how busy our service is and the speed of your internet connection. We work to deliver every result as quickly as we can. You will see a message on the screen when your result is ready.

If you have a problem

If you cannot download your document or do not receive your score, please write to support@plagifix.com or use our Contact page. We will help you get your result.
